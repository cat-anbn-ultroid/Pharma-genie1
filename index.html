The Problem

Standardized dosing ignores how Indian genomes differ.

Routine prescribing in India still relies on empirical, one-size-fits-all protocols derived from Caucasian clinical trial cohorts. These protocols overlook the distinct allele frequencies of high-risk pharmacogenes across diverse Indian sub-populations.

The result is a fundamental genomic mismatch: treatment failures and preventable toxicities that surface only after the damage is done. Without point-of-care genomic translation and drug-drug-gene interaction screening, tertiary centers are left to manage that risk by trial and error.

How It Works

From raw genotype to a safe prescription in four steps.

Designed for point-of-care workflows, so results arrive where prescribing decisions are made.

01

Upload Genomic Data

Accept patient VCF files, JSON diagnostic lab outputs, or individual genotype calls.

02

Private, Client-Side Processing

WebAssembly analysis runs in the browser, keeping sensitive genetic data on the device.

03

Risk Calibration

A Bayesian engine with phenoconversion and polypharmacy DDGI screening scores each drug.

04

Precision Recommendation

Sub-population-tailored dosing protocols and safer therapeutic alternatives, with an NNT calculator.
